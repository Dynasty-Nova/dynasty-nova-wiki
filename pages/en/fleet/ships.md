# Ships list

> **In short**: Dynasty Nova has 14 ships, built at the Orbital Dock. Civil ships carry, colonize, recycle or spy; combat ships attack and defend. This page gives each ship's cost, requirements and stats.
{.is-info}

> Ships are not translated in the game yet: it shows their French name (in brackets below) in every language. The English names used here are provisional.
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. Ships are built at the **Orbital Dock**. They are **paid per unit when queued** for production.
2. The game sorts them into three families: **civil** ships, **combat** ships and **special** ships.
3. Every ship has a **fixed drive**. Its speed rises with that drive's research: +10% per level of Combustion Drive, +20% per level of Impulse Drive, +30% per level of Hyperspace Drive. A ship never switches drives.
4. A fleet flies at the speed of its slowest ship.
5. **Cargo** capacity rises by 5% per level of Hyperspace Technology.
6. In combat, attack, shield and structure rise by 10% per level of Weapon Systems, Shielding Technology and Armor Technology. The combat **hull** equals structure divided by 10.
7. **Points**: each ship is worth 1 point per 1,000 resources of its cost. Combat and special ships count towards military points. The Cargo Shuttle, Stellar Freighter, Space Pioneer, Salvager, Scout and Solar Collector do not.
8. A destroyed ship is lost. The Repair Station can recover part of them after a battle lost while defending.

## Worked example

**A fleet of 10 Stellar Freighters with Hyperspace Technology 3**
- Cargo per Freighter: 25,000 × (1 + 0.05 × 3) = 28,750.
- Fleet cargo: 10 × 28,750 = **287,500 resources**.

**An Interceptor with Combustion Drive 6, Weapon Systems 2, Shielding Technology 0, Armor Technology 3**
- Speed: 12,500 × (1 + 0.1 × 6) = **20,000**.
- Attack: 50 × 1.2 = **60**.
- Shield: 10 × 1 = **10**.
- Combat hull: 4,000 × 1.3 / 10 = **520**.

### Solar Collector

The Solar Collector does not move and barely fights: it stays in orbit and produces energy. Its output depends on the planet's maximum temperature (Tmax / 4, plus a base amount of energy): the hotter the planet, the more it produces. Example seen in game: on a planet peaking at 60 °C, each Collector produces **35 energy**. It can be destroyed in an attack.

## Common pitfalls

- **Scouts and Solar Collectors attract rapid fire**: almost every ship has rapid fire 5 against them (see [Rapid fire](/en/fleet/rapid-fire)).
- **A weak shot bounces**: a shot below 1% of the target's shield has no effect. An Interceptor's attack (50) cannot dent a Stellar Colossus (shield 50,000, threshold 500).
- **The Stellar Colossus is very slow**: base speed 100. Any fleet that includes one moves at its pace.
- **Drives never change**: the Cargo Shuttle keeps the Combustion Drive whatever your Impulse Drive level.
- **The warp factor has no effect**: the game displays it, but no calculation uses it.
- **Cargo is not only for transport**: it also caps plunder and recycling.
