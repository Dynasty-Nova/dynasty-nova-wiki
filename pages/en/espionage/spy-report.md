# Reading a spy report

> **In short**: a spy report is split into sections: planet, resources, fleet, defenses and missiles, buildings, research. A missing section means your espionage level was too low, not that the target has nothing.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. The report always shows the **planet**: owner, coordinates, type, size and temperatures.
2. Other sections appear depending on your **effective level** (see [Spying](/en/espionage/spying)):
   - **Resources** from 1: metal, crystal, hydrogen on site;
   - **Fleet** from 3: ships in orbit;
   - **Defenses and missiles** from 5;
   - **Buildings** from 7;
   - **Research** from 9.
3. A section **not obtained** is not shown: the target may well have a fleet even if the Fleet section is missing.
4. A report on a player **on vacation** is empty.
5. A report can be **shared** with your alliance (see [Messages and reports](/en/players/messages-and-reports)) and **imported** into the [battle simulator](/en/combat/simulator).

## Worked example

Your report shows 120,000 metal, 80,000 crystal and 30,000 hydrogen, but no Fleet section (effective level 2).
- Possible loot on victory: 60,000 metal, 40,000 crystal, 15,000 hydrogen, so **115,000**: plan 5 Stellar Freighters (125,000 cargo).
- The target's fleet remains unknown: send probes again, or raise your Espionage Technology, before attacking.

## Common pitfalls

- **Missing section does not mean nothing**: never conclude a planet is undefended because the section is missing.
- **Reports get stale**: the target produces, builds and moves its fleet. Spy right before attacking.
- **An empty report may mean vacation**: check the status in the galaxy view.
