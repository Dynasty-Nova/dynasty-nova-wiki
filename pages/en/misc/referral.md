# Referral

> **In short**: invite players with your referral link. When a referee reaches 100 points in a universe, you receive 30 Stellar Points.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Every player has a **referral link** on the Referral page.
2. When a referee reaches **100 points** in a universe, you receive **30 Stellar Points**.
3. **Multi-accounting is forbidden**: referring yourself with a second account is not allowed.

## Worked example

Three friends join the game through your link and each reach 100 points: you receive 3 × 30 = **90 Stellar Points**.

## Common pitfalls

- **The reward is not immediate**: it arrives when the referee reaches 100 points, not when they sign up.
