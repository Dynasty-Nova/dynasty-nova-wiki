# Private universes

> **In short**: you can open your own paid Dynasty Nova universe, to play with friends, a guild or a community. You choose its size, speeds and rules, invite whoever you want, and run it from a creator console.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Creating a universe
1. Three plans are available: up to **25**, **75** or **200 players**, with speeds up to **×5**, **×10** or **×20** depending on the plan.
2. You set the map size, the five speeds (economy, buildings, research, shipyard, fleets) and the rules: beginner protection, attack limit, debris rates, moon formation, planet abandonment, queue size, bookmarks.
3. You set the **opening date**. Until it opens, everything can still be changed, and the date can be moved or brought forward.
4. Players join with an **invitation code** or a link. Generating a new code invalidates the old one, without affecting players already in.

### During the game
5. From the **creator console**, the maximum number of players changes immediately. Speeds and rules only change **24 hours later**, and the change is announced to every player.
6. A **public log** lists every change made by the creator or the administration, with the previous value. Every player in the universe can read it.
7. The creator can **exclude a player**. Exclusion is final: their planets are abandoned, their fleets in flight are lost, they leave their alliance and can never come back. It is written in the public log.
8. The creator can **withdraw shop items** from their universe: a withdrawn item can be neither bought nor used there.

### Payment and end
9. The subscription can be cancelled at any time. If a payment fails, the creator has **3 days** to settle it; otherwise the universe is **frozen** for everyone, like a general vacation, and resumes exactly as it was once paid. A frozen universe is **deleted 90 days later**.
10. A universe can also be paid for a fixed term. On its end date it stays viewable with a frozen final ranking, and can be extended by paying for the added days.

## Worked example

On Monday at 20:00, you raise your universe's fleet speed from ×2 to ×3:
- players are told immediately, and the change appears in the public log;
- the new speed applies on **Tuesday at 20:00**.

## Common pitfalls

- **Rules do not change right away**: allow 24 hours between the decision and its effect.
- **Exclusion cannot be undone**: the excluded player loses their planets and fleets in flight.
- **Watch the payment**: after 3 unpaid days, the game is frozen for every player.
