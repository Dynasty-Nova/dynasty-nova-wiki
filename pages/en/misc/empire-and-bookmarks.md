# Empire view and bookmarks

> **In short**: **bookmarks** star any planet or moon in the galaxy, with a private note. The **Empire view**, a Premium feature, sums up all your planets on a single page.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: the number of bookmarks may differ from one universe to another.
> Default and Redline: **10 bookmarks**, **200 with Premium**
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Bookmarks
1. A **star** in the galaxy view adds a planet or a moon to your bookmarks, with an optional **private note**.
2. The **Bookmarks** page (in the menu, next to Reports) shows each bookmark's current occupant, the editable note, and shortcuts to the galaxy, fleet dispatch and quick spy.
3. When sending a fleet, you can pick the destination from your bookmarks, in a side panel with search.
4. In the orbit view, a star marks bookmarked bodies.
5. Limit: **10 bookmarks** on a standard account, **200** with Premium.

### Empire view
6. The Empire view gathers, for **all your planets**, stocks, running constructions, fleets and what needs your attention.
7. It is a **Premium** feature.

## Worked example

With a standard account, you keep 10 targets bookmarked. With Premium, you can keep **200**, twenty times more.

## Common pitfalls

- **The occupant changes**: a bookmark keeps the position, not the player. The Bookmarks page shows the current occupant.
- **The note is private**: only you can see it.
