# Maintenance

> **In short**: during an announced maintenance, a banner appears and a **truce** applies on every universe: espionage, attacks and missile strikes are suspended. The rest of the game carries on as usual.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Maintenance is **announced** in advance by a banner.
2. During the maintenance window, **espionage, attacks and missile strikes are suspended** for everyone, on every universe.
3. **The rest of the game carries on**: production, constructions, research, transports.
4. **Fleets in flight** arrive normally, including attacks launched before maintenance began: the battle does take place. Only new departures are blocked.

## Worked example

Maintenance is announced from 06:00 to 06:30. At 06:10, you can neither launch an attack, nor spy, nor fire missiles. Your construction ending at 06:15 does end at 06:15.

## Common pitfalls

- **Do not plan an attack during the window**: it could not be launched.
- **The truce does not stop attacks already under way**: a fleet launched just before maintenance arrives normally.
- **Your economy does not stop**: use the time to manage constructions and transports.
