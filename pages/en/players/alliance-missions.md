# Alliance missions and station

> **In short**: an alliance levels up through its members' experience. Each level grants doctrine points, which buy talents: output, speed, fuel, research, combat, espionage, seats. Alliance missions set a shared goal each cycle and reward every member according to their contribution.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Treasury
1. The alliance has a **shared treasury** filled by members' donations.
2. The treasury cap is **5,000,000 per resource**, doubled per level of the Shared holds talent (up to 40,000,000).
3. A member can **request aid** from the treasury, within a daily cap (raised by 50,000 per level of the Assistance network talent). It is a helping hand, not an income.

### Station and missions
4. The **alliance station** must be built first, paid by the treasury. Missions then pay their experience into it.
5. Each cycle, a board proposes **missions**. The Coordinator (or any member with the "Manage missions" right) picks one and its difficulty, for the whole cycle. Striking a proposal costs resources from the treasury.
6. Every member **contributes by playing**. Only actions taken after the mission is picked and before the cycle ends count.
7. The mission **succeeds** as soon as the bar reaches the target. If the cycle ends first, it fails and pays nothing.
8. Based on their share, each member ranks **Bronze, Silver or Gold**. On success, they receive their tier's resource pack in their inventory, sometimes with Stellar Points.

### Level, doctrine and talents
9. The alliance earns **experience** from its members. Going from level n to the next costs 10,000 + 2,500 × (n − 1) experience points.
10. Each level grants **doctrine points**: 2 per level from level 1 to 5, then 1 per level, up to 50 points at level 45.
11. A talent level costs **1 doctrine point and 300,000 metal, 150,000 crystal, 50,000 hydrogen**, doubled at each level. Exception: both Doctrines have a single level, costing 2 points, 3,000,000 metal, 1,500,000 crystal and 500,000 hydrogen.
12. Talent bonuses apply to members present for more than **24 hours**.

## Worked example

**Raising the alliance from level 1 to level 5**
- Experience cost: 10,000 + 12,500 + 15,000 + 17,500 = **55,000**.
- Doctrine points at level 5: 5 × 2 = **10**.

**Coordinated extraction talent at level 3**
- Cost: 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 doctrine points, and 300,000 + 600,000 + 1,200,000 = **2,100,000 metal** (plus crystal and hydrogen).
- Effect: **+15%** metal, crystal and hydrogen output for every member.

### What earns the alliance experience

| Member action | Experience |
|---|---|
| Building a level | 20 × level |
| Completing a research level | 40 × level |
| Winning a raid | 5 per 1,000 military points destroyed |
| Spy report | 10 |
| Recycling | 2 per 1,000 resources |
| Alliance missions | depends on the mission |

Cap over a rolling 7 days: **800 per member** and **15,000 per alliance**.

### Alliance mission types

| Mission | What counts |
|---|---|
| Battles | Points of enemy ships and defenses destroyed (1 point per 1,000 resources), for the winner only; a draw counts for nothing |
| Plunder | Resources brought back from a won raid |
| Espionage | Each report counts as one base, once per mission; probes shot down do not count |
| Recycling | Resources harvested, including from your own battles |
| Donations | Resources given to the treasury, minus aid received during the mission |
| Buildings | Points of levels completed during the mission |
| Research | Points of research completed during the mission |
| Ships | Points of ships leaving the shipyard |
| Defenses | Points of defenses delivered |

## Common pitfalls

- **Unfinished work does not count**: a level or research still running when the cycle ends brings nothing to the mission.
- **Doctrines cost 2 points**: plan for them in your talent build.
- **The experience cap slows big players**: 800 per member over 7 days, anything beyond does not count.
- **New members wait 24 hours** before benefiting from talents.
