# Inactivity

> **In short**: a player who does not log in for 14 days loses beginner protection and can be attacked by anyone. There is no other threshold: no account deletion, no automatic planet abandonment.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: the inactivity delay may differ from one universe to another.
> Default and Redline: **14 days**
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. After **14 days without logging in**, a player loses [beginner protection](/en/players/beginner-protection): anyone can attack them, whatever the points gap.
2. It is the **only threshold**: the account is not deleted, and planets are not abandoned automatically.
3. [Vacation mode](/en/players/vacation-mode) stops the counter: inactivity does not build up during vacation.
4. In an alliance, a **founder** who stops logging in is warned at 7 then 12 days and **loses the seat at 14 days**, to a successor.

## Worked example

You have 300 points and leave for three weeks without turning on vacation mode. On day 15, a 50,000-point player, normally blocked by the gap, can attack you.

## Common pitfalls

- **Prepare a long absence**: turn on vacation mode, or you lose your protection after 14 days.
- **Alliance founders, beware**: after 14 days, your seat goes to another member.
