# Vacation mode

> **In short**: vacation mode pauses your empire in a universe. No more production, but nobody can attack you. It lasts at least 48 hours and has no maximum.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Vacation mode is turned on **per universe**, from your settings.
2. **To start it**: no fleet in flight, no construction, research or unit production running, no incoming attack.
3. **Minimum length: 48 hours.** After that, you come back whenever you want. There is **no maximum, no automatic end and no delay** between two vacation periods.
4. **While on vacation**: your mines produce nothing, and all your actions are refused (fleets, missiles, constructions, demolitions, Phalanx).
5. **Other players** can neither attack you, nor destroy your moon, nor transport resources to you, nor fire missiles at you.
6. **Espionage** and **recycling** of your debris fields remain possible, but a spy report on you is **empty**.
7. Your **inactivity does not build up** while on vacation.
8. Your planets show the "On vacation" status in the galaxy view.

## Worked example

You leave on Friday at 18:00:
- You can come back from **Sunday 18:00** (48 h).
- Coming back on Saturday is not possible: the minimum has not passed.
- If you stay away three weeks, nothing changes: no limit, and your inactivity counter does not run.

## Common pitfalls

- **Debris can still be recycled**: a debris field above your planet can be collected by others while you are away.
- **No production**: your mines do not work during vacation.
- **Prepare your departure**: finish or cancel constructions and research, and bring your fleets home, or vacation mode is refused.
