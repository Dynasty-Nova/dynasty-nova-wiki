# Messages and reports

> **In short**: the game includes a three-channel chat (general, alliance, private) and a Reports page that gathers your espionage, battle and mission reports. A report or a position can be shared with your alliance.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Chat
1. Three channels: **general** (all players in the universe), **alliance** (your alliance members) and **private** (a conversation with one player).
2. An **anti-flood** limits messages sent too close together. You can **block** a player to stop receiving their messages.
3. **Notifications** flag new messages, mentions and events.

### Reports
4. Each espionage, battle or mission produces a **report** on the Reports page.
5. You can **delete** a whole category of reports. This deletion is **permanent**.
6. **Sharing a report** with your alliance makes it readable by all its members, current and future, from the alliance channel. The message only carries a link to the report: everyone opens it with their own rights. It stays **30 days** in the channel.
7. A report shared with you belongs to its recipient: you can neither delete nor re-share it.
8. You can also **share a galaxy position** with your alliance (see [The galaxy view](/en/universe/galaxy-view)).
9. **Reports your way**: in Settings, pick the reports you receive and the ones the bell announces. Fleet returns are set mission by mission. Spy, battle and incident reports (fleet lost, failed colonization) are always received.
10. You can write to a player straight from the galaxy, a planet's sheet or the ranking.

## Worked example

You share a spy report on the 1st of the month: your allies can open it from the alliance channel until at least the 31st, even if you remove it from your history in the meantime.

## Common pitfalls

- **Deleting a category erases everything**: there is no recycle bin.
- **A shared report stays visible**: even removed from your history, it remains available to the alliance for 30 days.
- **A spy report on a player on vacation is empty**: this is not a bug.
