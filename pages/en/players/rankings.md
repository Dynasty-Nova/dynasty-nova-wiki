# Rankings and points

> **In short**: every resource you invest earns points: 1 point per 1,000 resources. There are four rankings: economy (buildings), research, military (combat ships, defenses, missiles) and the general ranking, which adds up the three.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. **1 point = 1,000 resources spent** (metal + crystal + hydrogen). Energy does not count.
2. **Economy**: building levels.
3. **Research**: technology levels.
4. **Military**: combat and special ships, all defenses and all missiles.
5. Not counted as military: Cargo Shuttle, Stellar Freighter, Space Pioneer, Salvager, Scout, Solar Collector.
6. **General** = economy + research + military.
7. Points **go down** when you lose units, fire missiles or demolish a building level.
8. Your points set your [beginner protection](/en/players/beginner-protection) tier.

## Worked example

- You raise the Mineral Excavator from level 13 to 14 for 11,677 metal and 2,919 crystal: **+14.6 economy points**.
- You build a Battleship (45,000 metal, 15,000 crystal): **+60 military points**.
- You build 10 Stellar Freighters (12,000 resources each): **+0 military points**, since they are civil ships.
- Your Battleship is destroyed in combat: **−60 military points**.

## Detailed data

| Ranking | What counts | What lowers it |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Building levels | Demolition |
| Research | Technology levels | |
| Military | Combat and special ships, defenses, missiles | Destroyed units, missiles fired |
| General | Sum of the three | All of the above |

## Common pitfalls

- **Transport ships do not count**: a big fleet of Stellar Freighters does not raise your military ranking.
- **Neither does the Solar Collector**, although it is listed among special ships.
- **Climbing the ranking changes your protection**: crossing a point tier changes who you can attack and who can attack you.
