# Alliances

> **In short**: an alliance brings players together under one banner, with a chat channel, a shared treasury, talents and collective missions. Founding one requires the Stellar Diplomacy technology. Its size depends on its founder's Stellar Diplomacy level.
{.is-info}

<img src="/images/illustrations/research/diplomatie-stellaire.jpg" width="180" alt="Stellar Diplomacy">

## Rules

### Founding
1. Founding an alliance requires **Stellar Diplomacy level 1** (so Quantum Computing 4 and Innovation Center 5).
2. An alliance has a **name** and a **tag**.
3. **Capacity** depends on the **founder's** Stellar Diplomacy level: from 10 members at level 1 to 50 at level 8. A capacity once reached never goes down. The **Embassies** talent adds 2 seats per level (3 levels), so **56 members** at most.

### Joining and leaving
4. You can **apply** to as many alliances as you like and receive several **invitations**, which never expire. Joining an alliance cancels all other requests.
5. **Settling-in period**: during their **first 24 hours**, a new member holds a seat but gets none of the alliance's benefits.
6. After **leaving** an alliance or being **excluded**, you must wait **24 hours** before joining another. Coming back later starts from scratch: seniority and settling-in period included.

### The founder
7. The founder **cannot leave** the alliance: they must first hand the founding seat to another member, and then become vice-president. This choice is final.
8. **Automatic succession**: a founder who stops logging in is warned at 7 then 12 days, and **loses the seat at 14 days**. The successor is chosen by rank, then by seniority in that rank. They must be present and a member for more than 24 hours.

### Running the alliance
9. Members get **ranks** and **functions** (for example managing missions, voting on wars).
10. Changing the **name and tag** (together) or the **description** costs Stellar Points. The former name stays visible in the alliance's public history.
11. **Disbanding** the alliance loses everything for good (treasury, station, talents, prestige, cosmetics). The name and tag stay reserved for 30 days.
12. **Announcement**: the founder and the vice-president can pin a message at the top of the alliance home, visible to members only.

## Worked example

The founder has Stellar Diplomacy level 5 and the alliance has the Embassies talent at level 2:
- Capacity: 30 (Stellar Diplomacy 5) + 2 × 2 (Embassies) = **34 members**.
- If the founder hands the seat to a member with only Stellar Diplomacy 1, the capacity already reached does not go down.

## Common pitfalls

- **Pick the founder carefully**: alliance size depends on their research, not the members'.
- **The 24 hours matter**: a new member gets neither alliance bonuses nor the right to inherit the founding seat during their first 24 hours.
- **Switching alliances costs time**: 24 hours of waiting, and your seniority starts over.
- **A disbanded alliance cannot be recovered**: everything it built is lost.
