# Pacts, fair play and wars

> **In short**: alliances can sign pacts (non-aggression or alliance) and declare war on each other by vote. Neither pacts nor wars change combat rules: what is at stake is the alliance's word, measured by its fair-play score.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Pacts
1. Two kinds of pact: **non-aggression** and **alliance**.
2. A pact binds nobody until **the other alliance has signed it**.
3. **Attacking** a member of an alliance bound by a pact is still possible, but the attack is recorded as a **violation** and costs your alliance fair-play points: **10 points** for a non-aggression pact, **15 points** for an alliance pact. Recalling the fleet does not undo the violation.
4. **Denouncing** a pact is public and cannot be undone. The pact still binds for **24 hours** after denunciation: attacking during that notice is still a violation.
5. Breaking a pact cleanly costs nothing, but takes those 24 hours of notice.

### Fair play
6. The fair-play score measures what the alliance's word is worth.
7. A violation lowers it; each day of pacts kept earns **1 point**, plus 1 per level of the **Kept word** talent.

### Wars
8. A war is decided by a **vote**. Opening a question requires the diplomacy right; officers and above vote.
9. The question is adopted by a **majority of the votes cast**. A tie rejects it. Adoption declares war immediately.
10. The war becomes **mutual** once both sides have declared it. It ends with an **accepted peace**.
11. War counts victories and marks enemy cells on the map. It **changes nothing** about combat, plunder or the [attack limit](/en/players/attack-limit).

## Worked example

Your alliance has a non-aggression pact with alliance B and the Kept word talent at level 2.
- Each day the pact is kept: 1 + 2 = **+3 fair-play points**.
- One of your members attacks a player from B: **−10 points**, even if they recall the fleet.

## Common pitfalls

- **The 24-hour notice applies**: denouncing a pact and attacking right away is still a violation.
- **Recalling erases nothing**: the violation is recorded as soon as the attack is launched.
- **War grants no extra rights**: the 6 attacks per day limit and beginner protection still apply.
