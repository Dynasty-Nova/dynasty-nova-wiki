# Tech tree

> **In short**: most buildings, technologies, ships and defenses require certain levels elsewhere first. For the most sought-after goals, this page lists everything you need to reach, then what each building and technology unlocks.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. A requirement is a **minimum level** of a building or a technology.
2. Until a requirement is met, the item stays locked in the game.
3. Requirements chain: a required technology has its own requirements. The tables below give the **full list**, chains included.

## Worked example

To build your first **Space Pioneer** and colonize, you need in total:
- Buildings: Innovation Center 3, Automaton Factory 2, Orbital Dock 4.
- Technologies: Energy Science 1, Combustion Drive 3, Impulse Drive 3, Espionage Technology 4, Astrophysics 1.

## Common pitfalls

- **Espionage Technology leads to colonization**: level 4 is needed for Astrophysics, and so for the Space Pioneer.
- **The Innovation Center is the real lock**: many technologies need a Center at level 7, 10 or 12. Level 12 is also the maximum.
- **Hyperspace Technology opens heavy propulsion**: without Hyperspace Technology 3, no Hyperspace Drive, so no Battleship.
- **Quantum Computing leads to alliances**: its level 4 is required for Stellar Diplomacy, itself required to found an alliance.
