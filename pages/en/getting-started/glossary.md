# Glossary and naming

> **In short**: this page lists every resource, building, research, ship, defense and mission under the name the game displays, in English, French and Spanish.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. The names below are **the ones the game displays**, recorded on 2 October 2026. The wiki uses them as they are, even when they differ between languages (for example "Laser Technology" in English, "Systèmes Photoniques" in French).
2. The **code** is the unit's technical identifier. It does not change with the language, which makes it handy to match names across languages or in the simulator.
3. **Ships are not translated yet**: the game shows their French name in every language. Until official names exist, the English wiki uses provisional translations, shown in italics.

## Common pitfalls

- **Interceptor or Interception Missile?** The Interceptor is the first combat ship. The Interception Missile (shown as "Interceptors" in the game) is the missile that destroys Long-Range Warheads.
- **Codes do not follow names**: `battle_cruiser` is the Stellar Colossus, not a cruiser; `shipyard` is the Orbital Dock.
- **English research names** are not translations of the French ones. To switch languages, rely on the code.
- **Ship names in game**: while ships are untranslated, look for the French name in the game screens.
