# First steps

> **In short**: you start with a mother planet, a few resources and 7 days of protection. Follow the quests: they make you build in the right order and give you resources. Keep an eye on three things: energy, storage and your protection.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: starting resources and the length of new player protection may differ from one universe to another.
> Redline: **500 metal and 500 crystal** to start, **7 days** of protection
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. When you join a universe, you get a **mother planet** with **163 fields**, placed preferably in the middle of its system, with a **Planetary Base** already built.
2. You are **protected from attacks for 7 days** (new player protection). The remaining time shows at the top of the screen. Launching an attack or a missile salvo ends it for good.
3. On top of that, **beginner protection** based on points applies: a player much stronger or much weaker than you cannot attack you (see [Beginner protection](/en/players/beginner-protection)).
4. Resources (metal, crystal, hydrogen) are produced **continuously** by your mines, even while you are offline.
5. **Quests** give the recommended build order and resource packs (see [The tutorial](/en/getting-started/tutorial-quests)).

## Worked example

Your first builds, following the grade 1 quests:
1. Mineral Excavator 1, then Crystal Extractor 1.
2. Photovoltaic Sensors 1, to power both mines.
3. Mineral Excavator 3, Crystal Extractor 2, Photovoltaic Sensors 3.
4. Alloy Depot 1, Innovation Center 1, Hydrogen Condenser 1.

Completing grade 1 gives you 2,634 metal, 859 crystal, 200 hydrogen and 100 experience points.

## Detailed data

### Three early habits

| Habit | Why | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Positive energy | If your mines use more than your power plants produce, all output drops by the same proportion | Resource bar, "Energy shortage" line in the production breakdown |
| Storage not full | A resource whose stock exceeds capacity is no longer produced | Resource bar, storage buildings |
| Protection | For 7 days nobody can attack you, unless you attack first | Counter at the top of the screen |

### What comes next

| Step | Quest grade | What you unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Research | 2 | Energy Science, Quantum Computing (fleet slots) |
| Shipyard | 2 | Orbital Dock, Cargo Shuttle, Ballistic Projector |
| Espionage | 3 | Scout |
| Heavy transport | 4 | Stellar Freighter |
| Colonization | 5 | Space Pioneer, first colony |
| Recycling | 6 | Salvager, Repair Station |

## Common pitfalls

- **Attacking ends your protection**: the first attack or missile salvo you launch removes your 7 days of protection, with no way back. Spying and transport do not remove it.
- **Do not raise mines without power plants**: an energy shortage slows everything down.
- **Mind your storage**: beyond capacity, production stops.
- **Secure your resources before going away**: once protection is over, stored resources can be plundered. [Vacation mode](/en/players/vacation-mode) pauses your empire.
