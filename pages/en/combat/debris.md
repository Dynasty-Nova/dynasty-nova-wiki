# Debris fields

> **In short**: when ships are destroyed, 30% of their metal and crystal forms a debris field in orbit. Debris never disappears: it must be recycled with Salvagers. A large debris field can also give birth to a moon.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: the debris rate of ships and defenses, and the moon formation chance and threshold, may differ from one universe to another.
> Default and Redline: ships **30%**, defenses **0%**, moon **20% maximum for 2,000,000 debris**
{.is-info}

<img src="/images/illustrations/ships/recuperateur.jpg" width="180" alt="Salvager">

## Rules

1. Destroyed ships **on both sides**, civil ones included, leave **30%** of their cost in **metal and crystal**. Hydrogen leaves nothing.
2. Destroyed **defenses** leave **no debris**.
3. Battles against expedition pirates and aliens leave **10%**.
4. Debris **never disappears**. Only recycling removes it. It stays even if the planet below disappears.
5. To collect it, send **Salvagers** on a **Recycling** mission. The haul is capped by the fleet's cargo: whatever does not fit stays in orbit.
6. **Moon formation**: after a battle, the chance that a moon forms is **1% per 100,000 debris**, up to **20%** (reached at 2,000,000 debris). Formation does not consume the debris.
7. Destroying a moon leaves no debris.

## Worked example

You destroy 100 Interceptors (3,000 metal and 1,000 crystal each):
- Debris: 30% × 300,000 metal = **90,000 metal**, and 30% × 100,000 crystal = **30,000 crystal**.
- Total haul: 120,000 resources. A Salvager carries 20,000: you need **6** (without Hyperspace Technology bonus).
- Chance of forming a moon: 120,000 / 100,000 = **1.2%**.

## Detailed data

| Source | Share turned into debris |
|---|---|
| Destroyed ship (attacker or defender) | 30% of metal and crystal |
| Destroyed defense | 0% |
| Expedition battle (pirates, aliens) | 10% |
| Moon destruction | nothing |

| Battle debris | 100,000 | 500,000 | 1,000,000 | 2,000,000 or more |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Moon chance | 1% | 5% | 10% | 20% |

## Common pitfalls

- **Your own losses feed the field**: a battle lost while defending also leaves debris above your planet, which others can recycle.
- **Hyperspace Technology raises cargo**: +5% per level, handy to recycle with fewer Salvagers.
- **Defenses yield nothing**: only destroyed fleets create debris.
- **First come, first served**: the field belongs to whoever recycles it.
