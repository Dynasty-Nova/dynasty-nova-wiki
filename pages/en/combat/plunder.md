# Plunder

> **In short**: when the attacker wins a battle, they take **50% of each resource** stored on the planet, within the free cargo space of their surviving ships. If cargo space is too small, each resource is reduced by the same proportion.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: the plundered share may differ from one universe to another.
> Default and Redline: **50%** of each resource
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Plunder only happens **when the attacker wins**. A draw brings nothing.
2. The loot equals **50% of each resource** (metal, crystal, hydrogen) on the planet, rounded down. Energy is not plundered.
3. Loot is capped by the **free cargo space of surviving ships**, taking into account Hyperspace Technology (+5% per level) and what is already on board.
4. If the loot exceeds cargo space, **each resource is reduced by the same proportion**. No resource has priority.
5. The rate does not drop from one attack to the next: it is the target's stock that shrinks. Plundering the same player again therefore pays less and less if they do not produce in between.
6. Buildings, ships and defenses cannot be plundered.

## Worked example

The target stores 100,000 metal, 60,000 crystal and 20,000 hydrogen.
- Possible loot: 50,000 metal, 30,000 crystal, 10,000 hydrogen, so **90,000**.
- With 10 surviving Stellar Freighters (250,000 cargo): everything is taken.
- With only 45,000 free cargo, half the loot: **25,000 metal, 15,000 crystal and 5,000 hydrogen**.
- After a first full raid (90,000 taken), 50,000 metal, 30,000 crystal and 10,000 hydrogen remain: a second attack right after, with no production in between, can only take **45,000**.

## Common pitfalls

- **Bring cargo space**: combat ships carry little. Add Stellar Freighters to your attack fleet.
- **Losses shrink cargo space**: only surviving ships carry the loot.
- **A draw brings nothing**: you must wipe out the whole defense to plunder.
- **Switch targets**: each raid on the same player brings less than the last.
