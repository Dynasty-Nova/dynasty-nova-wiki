# Universes

> **In short**: Dynasty Nova is played in universes. Each universe is an independent game, with its own map, players, ranking and settings. You can play in several universes with the same account: your progress is kept separate.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Each universe is **independent**: planets, resources, fleets, ranking, alliances, quests and experience belong to it.
2. You arrive in each universe with a new mother planet and your own 7-day **new player protection**.
3. **Vacation mode** is turned on universe by universe.
4. A universe can announce an **opening date**: a countdown shows before it.
5. A universe that has reached its **end date** remains viewable, with a frozen final ranking.
6. Each universe has its own **settings** (table below). Wiki pages flag the values concerned with a "Universe setting" box.
7. The universe **type** (andromeda, orion, quantum, vega) and its visual variant are rolled at random when it is created. They only change the map's look.
8. You can also create your own **private universe**, with its players, speeds and rules (see [Private universes](/en/misc/private-universes)).

## Worked example

On Redline, all speeds are ×1. In a universe with building speed ×2, a building that takes 2 hours on Redline would only take one.

## Detailed data

### Universe settings

| Setting | Redline |
|---|---|
| Economy speed (production) | ×1 |
| Building speed | ×1 |
| Research speed | ×1 |
| Shipyard speed | ×1 |
| Fleet speed | ×1 |
| Galaxies × systems × positions | 9 × 99 × 15 |
| Maximum players | 1,000 |
| Starting resources | 500 metal, 500 crystal |
| Beginner protection | 1:3 under 500 points, 1:5 under 5,000, 1:10 under 500,000 |
| Inactivity lifting protection | 14 days |
| New player protection | 7 days |
| Attack limit | 6 per 24 h |
| Debris | ships 30%, defenses 0% |
| Moon formation | 20% maximum for 2,000,000 debris |
| Plunder | 50% of each resource |
| Build queues | 2 orders, 5 with Premium |
| Bookmarks | 10, 200 with Premium |
| Planet abandonment | disappears after 60 days, former owner barred from recolonizing for 30 days |

## Common pitfalls

- **Nothing carries over between universes**: resources, fleets and alliances stay in their universe.
- **Check the settings**: a wiki figure flagged "Universe setting" may differ in your universe.
